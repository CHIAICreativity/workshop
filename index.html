Workshop Proposal

Breaking the Mold: Evaluating and Fostering Diversity and Creativity in AI-Generated Ideas

Abstract

Generative AI can produce ideas at unprecedented scale, yet generating more ideas does not necessarily increase diversity or novelty. Homogeneity and abundance can make meaningful differences and promising directions difficult to identify. How can we help people use generative models to move beyond familiar patterns and generate “out-of-distribution” ideas that are diverse, novel, and useful?

This workshop addresses this challenge through three complementary perspectives:

  1. Idea evaluation: identifying homogeneity and assessing diversity, novelty, and usefulness in alignment with human experts’ judgments;
  2. Idea generation: using these evaluations to guide models toward more diverse and novel generation; and
  3. Idea consumption: designing interfaces and feedback mechanisms that help people explore, assess, and develop ideas collaboratively with AI across science, engineering, design, and content creation.

Through discussion and collaborative activities, participants will develop a research agenda connecting these perspectives to support innovative idea generation.
